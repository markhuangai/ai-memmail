# ai-memmail

Email processing, conversation history, and manual handling of managed mailboxes.

## Language

**Thread handoff**:
The transfer of a managed conversation to a personal inbox for manual handling, including its transcript and subsequent inbound messages.

**Handoff transcript**:
The ordered inbound and outbound messages sent when handing a conversation to a personal inbox.

**Remote target**:
The correspondent whom the recipient of a handoff should address when replying.
